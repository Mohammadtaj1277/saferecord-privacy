SafeRecord – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SafeRecord (“the app”) is developed by Md Taj (“I”, “me”). Contact: tajtech.studio@gmail.com

What the app does

SafeRecord records video with sound on your phone when you tap Record. A notification is shown the whole time it records. If you choose, it backs the recording up to your own Google Drive. When you use the HELP / Emergency button, it prepares a message with your location and a video link, which you send yourself through WhatsApp or your SMS app.

Information the app uses

  • Video and audio – recorded by the camera and microphone only while you record. Stored on your phone (inside the app and, after you stop, in your gallery folder DCIM/SafeRecord).
  • Precise location (GPS) – used only while you use the app (recording or HELP), to write the time and place on the video, to save the position in the video file details, and to add a map link to your HELP message.
  • Place name – to show “Near <road/area>”, the app asks the Android / Google geocoding service to convert your coordinates into a place name. This needs internet and sends your coordinates to that service.
  • Emergency contact phone numbers and your name – typed by you and stored only on your phone.
  • Google account email – only if you link Google Drive; used to upload your recordings to your Drive.

Where data goes

  • Videos stay on your phone. If you link Google Drive, they are uploaded to a folder in your own Google Drive. I run no server and I cannot see, copy or access your videos, location or contacts.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I do not sell or share your data.
  • The HELP message is sent by you, using WhatsApp or your SMS app, to the contacts you choose. The app cannot send messages by itself.

Google user data

The app asks Google for one permission only: drive.file. With it the app can see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself in your Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files. SafeRecord’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Shared Drive link

So that family can watch what is happening, the app makes the Drive folder of a recording view-only for anyone who has the link. Anyone you (or they) send the link to can open it. You can switch this off at any time with the “Link band” button on a recording in the app’s Vault, or in Google Drive’s sharing settings.

Permissions

  • Camera / Microphone – to record video with sound.
  • Location – to add time, place and a map link. Only while you use the app; the app does not collect location in the background when it is not recording.
  • Notifications – to show that recording is running.
  • Foreground service (camera, microphone, location) – lets recording continue when the screen is off. A notification is always visible; the app never records secretly.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save the finished video in your gallery.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any recording from the app’s Vault. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Files already uploaded to Google Drive, and copies saved in your gallery, stay until you delete them yourself. To ask me anything about your data, write to tajtech.studio@gmail.com.

Children

SafeRecord is not meant for children under 13.

Responsible use and limits

You are responsible for following the laws about recording people where you live. SafeRecord is not a replacement for the police or the 112 emergency number, and it does not guarantee any outcome or the legal acceptance of a recording.
